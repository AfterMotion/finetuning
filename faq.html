Technical Staging & Production FAQ

Standard operating procedures, engineering safety factors, acoustic simulations, and equipment capabilities for corporate and public events.

🔍
We perform 3D acoustic ray-tracing using EASE and ArrayCalc software prior to load-in. Instead of blasting a single loud source that creates reflections off marble and glass, we deploy distributed line-array delay towers aligned to millisecond accuracy over redundant Dante IP networks. This maintains a STI (Speech Transmission Index) score ≥ 0.75 across every VIP seat.
Our Black Pearl P2.6 panels feature a 3,840 Hz refresh rate and 16-bit grayscale depth, making them 100% flicker-free for broadcast 4K cameras with zero moiré patterning at camera shutter angles down to 1/120s. The minimum optimal human eye viewing distance is 2.5 meters.
We enforce a minimum 5:1 working load limit (WLL) safety factor on all Prolyte H30V and black-anodized box trussing spans. Every suspension point is rigged using CM Lodestar electric hoists with secondary wire-rope safety bonds and live wireless load cells monitored at the FOH control desk.
All production hardware is isolated from municipal grid fluctuations. We supply dual synchronized sound-attenuated diesel generators operating in 100% N+1 auto-transfer mode. Critical FOH consoles and LED video processors are buffered by dual online double-conversion UPS units ensuring zero-millisecond power dropout.
Standard corporate summits require 10 to 14 days for site 3D surveying, CAD rigging plots, and audio simulations. Large architectural exhibition pavilions (such as Motor Fest BYD / Royal Enfield) require 21 to 30 days for CNC fabrication, structural box-truss engineering, and client CAD sign-off. Expedited 72-hour turnarounds are available for existing fleet setups.
