8th Motor Fest Chattogram: Engineering Luxury Staging for BYD and Royal Enfield

When international automotive brands present their latest flagships to the Bangladeshi public, their staging architecture must reflect precision engineering. For the 8th Motor Fest held at the GEC Convention Center in Chattogram, Fine Tuning took complete turnkey charge of pavilion architecture, structural carpentry, and ambient lighting for BYD Auto and Royal Enfield Bangladesh.

The BYD pavilion spotlighted the new energy electric Seal 5 and Sealion 6 SUV. To accentuate the vehicle lines, our lighting designers avoided direct harsh downlights, instead opting for high-CRI soft profiles suspended from overhead aluminum box trusses. Custom heavy-duty vehicle reveal platforms engineered with 3,500kg axle ratings ensured total stability throughout the three-day convention.

Concurrently, the Royal Enfield Pure Motorcycling display required an authentic vintage texture. Our master carpenters crafted weathered timber accents, illuminated 3D channel lettering, and warm 3200K tungsten spotlighting to match the heritage of the century-old motorcycle brand.
