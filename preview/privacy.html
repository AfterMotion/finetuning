Privacy Policy & Technical Data Protection

Effective Date: November 1, 2026 • Fine Tuning Advertising Agency

1. Scope & Jurisdiction

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, processing, and storage of technical project briefs, corporate identity files, CAD engineering plots, and VIP attendee telemetry handled by Fine Tuning Advertising Agency, operating registered production facilities in Chattogram and Dhaka, Bangladesh.

All activities comply with the Information and Communication Technology Act of Bangladesh and applicable international data governance standards for enterprise corporate engagements.

2. CAD & Proprietary Engineering Blueprints

All 3D architectural schematics, rigging calculations, acoustic ray-tracing plots, and custom stage designs prepared for clients (such as BSRM, UCB, BYD, Royal Enfield) remain protected trade secrets:

  • Client-provided venue blueprints and architectural files are stored in encrypted vaults and accessed solely by credentialed production directors.
  • Engineering calculations are retained for structural safety compliance audits for a period of five (5) years following event execution.
  • Fine Tuning will never license, transfer, or repurpose custom stage builds to competitor brands without express written authorization.

3. Strict Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) Guarantee

For sensitive product launches, corporate annual general meetings, and embargoed vehicle reveals, Fine Tuning executes formal bilateral Non-Disclosure Agreements prior to briefing receipt.

All production crew members, on-site riggers, LED technicians, and external security contractors sign binding confidentiality declarations prohibiting unapproved photography, social media leaks, or disclosure of embargoed presentation materials prior to official launch.

4. FOH Telemetry & Acoustic Logging

During live events, our Front-of-House (FOH) console monitors decibel levels (dBA/dBC), power stability, and Dante network packet flow. This telemetry is logged purely for safety, acoustic calibration, and load compliance. No private attendee conversations, off-mic audio, or non-public communications are recorded or archived.

5. Data Retention & Inquiries

Clients may request complete sanitization of digital presentation decks and unreleased assets immediately following post-production sign-off by contacting our compliance desk at finetuningbd@gmail.com.
