Brand Governance

Logo Usage: Dos & Don'ts

Strict visual rules to maintain brand integrity across all web and print collateral.

DO: Preserve Original Aspect Ratio

Always scale the logo proportionally. Ensure clear space equal to the height of the letter "F" on all four boundaries.

DO: Use Midnight Obsidian Backgrounds

Place the logo on dark stage backgrounds (#070D18 / #0B1320) or clean editorial white (#FFFFFF) with high contrast.

DON'T: Invert or Alter Colors

Never swap the needle to teal or the crescent to orange. Never apply unapproved purple or neon gradients.

DON'T: Distort, Rotate, or Skew

Never tilt the logo at an angle or apply heavy harsh drop-shadows that degrade optical crispness.
