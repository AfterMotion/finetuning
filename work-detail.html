8th Motor Fest, Chattogram — BYD & Royal Enfield Exhibition Pavilions

BYD Auto & Royal Enfield Bangladesh

Sector Category Automotive Exhibition
Event Date October 2026
Primary Venue GEC Convention Center, Chattogram
Attendance Scope 15,000+ Visitors

Project Brief & Execution

Fine Tuning engineered and fabricated custom architectural pavilions for global new energy vehicle manufacturer BYD (showcasing the Seal 5 sedan and Sealion 6 SUV) alongside an authentic heritage showcase for Royal Enfield ("Pure Motorcycling Since 1901").

Technical Staging Challenge

Fabricating heavy-duty vehicle turntables and platforms within a 24-hour setup window while ensuring ambient lighting complemented automotive paint finishes without glare.

Engineering Specifications

  • Custom vehicle reveal platforms with 3,500kg load test capacity
  • Overhead aluminum box trussing with 3200K warm and 5600K cool balanced profiles
  • 3D illuminated acrylic channel lettering and glowing neon typography
  • Integrated VIP client reception lounge and promotional booth
