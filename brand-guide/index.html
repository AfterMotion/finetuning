Design Governance

Do's & Don'ts Benchmark

Strict implementation rules for digital developers and agency creative directors.

✓

Approved Implementation (DO)

  • Direct WhatsApp Conversion: Prioritize instant WhatsApp communication with contextual inquiry pills.
  • High-Contrast Text Teal: Use `#2B5E70` for all light-mode teal text to ensure 7.15:1 WCAG AAA compliance.
  • Midnight Text on Amber Buttons: Always pair `#F09320` button fills with `#070D18` text for 8.24:1 AAA readability.
  • Spacious Photography: Feature dramatic high-impact stage photography from verified client productions.
  • Monospaced Telemetry: Render event countdown clocks, coordinates, and decibels in `JetBrains Mono`.
✕

Strict Violations (DON'T)

  • Zero Form Friction: Never trap prospective clients in long 10-field corporate contact forms.
  • No White Text on Amber: Never put white text over `#F09320` buttons (2.36:1 contrast violation).
  • No Raster Drop Shadows on Mark: Never add blurry black shadows directly to the SVG emblem.
  • No Stock Business Photos: Never use generic stock photography of handshakes or fake boardrooms.
  • No Corporate Clichés: Strictly avoid buzzwords like "one-stop-shop" or "synergistic paradigm".
Project Clarity

Assumptions & Open Questions

Preserving supplied facts while documenting design system assumptions for stakeholder signoff.

Documented Assumptions

1. Digital Modernist Typeface: While the original vector wordmark features flared serifs (`FineTuning`), we assume `Outfit` as the primary digital workhorse to ensure crisp rendering across mobile and desktop displays.

2. Text-Safe Contrast Adaptation: We introduce `--color-teal-700` (`#2B5E70`) to fulfill WCAG AAA text contrast standards on light canvases where the raw logo teal (`#4694B1`) falls short.

3. WhatsApp Primary Channel: In alignment with business practice across Bangladesh, direct WhatsApp chat is established as the primary conversion funnel over static web forms.

Open Questions for Stakeholders

1. Sub-Brand Monograms: Does Fine Tuning plan to formalize dedicated sub-brand marks (e.g. *Fine Tuning Media*, *Fine Tuning Tech / 360 Booth*) with color-coded emblem variants?

2. Simplified 16px Favicon: At 16px favicon resolution, the fine gap between the needle and the cradle arc can blend together on non-retina displays. Should a simplified optical favicon variant be rendered?

3. Official Bangla Wordmark: When co-branding in Bangla (e.g. government summits or university partnerships), should a bespoke Bangla wordmark (ফাইন টিউনিং) be drafted?
