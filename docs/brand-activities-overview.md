- Comprehensive delegate registration, corporate wayfinding standees, and VIP hospitality.
- *Benchmark Projects*: BSRM CORE ESG Event, International Conference on the Future of Business at Radisson Blu Chattogram.

### 2.2 Brand Activations & Exhibition Pavilions
- Custom architectural exhibition stalls, illuminated 3D channel lettering, and vehicle showcase platforms.
- Interactive brand experience zones, product reveal mechanics, and lighting rigs.
- *Benchmark Projects*: 8th Motor Fest Chattogram (BYD Seal 5 & Sealion 6 Pavilion, Royal Enfield Pure Motorcycling Stall, GP Lubricants / Bismillah Automobiles booth), Tealive Grand Opening.

### 2.3 Mega Reunions, Retreats & Cultural Celebrations

### 2.5 Big Screen Live Broadcasts & Stadium Screenings

- *Benchmark Projects*: FIFA World Cup Live Viewing Festivals across multiple venues (Galleria CPDL, BDR Field Halishahar, Chittagong University campus).

- *Benchmark Projects*: Lion Mohammad Amjad Hossain Chowdhury Biographical Documentary, Ctg 360 Photo Booth activations across weddings, family days, and conventions.
