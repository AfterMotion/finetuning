Master Staging & Event Production Terms

Version 2.5 • Fine Tuning Advertising Agency (Chattogram & Dhaka)

1. Scope of Turnkey Engagement

These Master Terms govern all equipment deployment, technical labor, CAD engineering, structural rigging, line-array audio reinforcement, and curved LED staging executed by Fine Tuning for commercial clients, corporate enterprises, and institutions.

2. Load-In, Structural Rigging & Safety Autonomy

Fine Tuning enforces a strict 5:1 working load limit safety factor on all overhead box-trussing installations. Our designated Lead Structural Rigging Inspector possesses full stop-work authority if:

  • Venue ceiling hang-points fail to provide certified civil engineering load ratings.
  • Unauthorized external electrical cables or untested third-party devices are connected to our synchronized power distribution boards.
  • Indoor ingress/egress pathways are obstructed in violation of Fire Service and Civil Defense regulations.

3. Equipment Custody & Site Security

Fine Tuning maintains all staging hardware in pristine calibrated condition. While on the event premises during rehearsal and show periods, the client or venue agrees to provide 24-hour perimeter security. Fine Tuning carries comprehensive equipment loss and damage insurance, excluding intentional sabotage or gross client negligence.

4. Outdoor Operations & Force Majeure Wind Protocols

For outdoor pavilions, open-air concerts, and sports activations, structural box-truss towers and LED arrays are rated for sustained wind speeds up to 40 km/h. If continuous gusts exceed 45 km/h or severe lightning strikes occur within a 5 km radius, Fine Tuning safety directors will immediately lower elevated truss rigs to ground lock position to ensure zero human injury risk.

5. Commercial Invoicing, Taxes & VAT

All production engagements are formally documented with stamped work orders. Standard payment schedules require 50% mobilization advance upon contract signing, 40% upon successful on-site staging rehearsal sign-off, and 10% upon event conclusion. All invoices are issued with formal Bangladesh Mushak-6.3 VAT documentation.
