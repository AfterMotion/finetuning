Our Heritage & Vision

CREATING EXPERIENCES. DELIVERING EXCELLENCE.

Fine Tuning was founded with a singular conviction: that experiential stagecraft in Bangladesh should rival the highest global engineering standards. Operating from active hubs in Chattogram and Dhaka, we transform complex corporate visions into seamless, broadcast-grade realities.

OUR STORY

What began as a specialized event production unit in Chattogram has matured into a nationwide experiential agency trusted by Bangladesh's premier industrial conglomerates, multinational automotive giants, and financial institutions.

From pioneering curved P2.6 LED volume integration to managing 2,000+ guest arena celebrations in remote lakeside valleys, our in-house engineering team owns every layer of production: civil rigging, structural carpentry, power distribution, acoustic alignment, and 4K multi-feed broadcasting.
